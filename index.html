One leg, built to walk.

Each leg is a complete assembly: a thigh and calf/shank, an ankle and a foot, joined by pins and links and driven by CubeMars AKE90-8 actuators.

  • Thigh, calf/shank, ankle and foot in a single assembly
  • 2 × CubeMars AKE90-8 actuator assemblies per leg
  • 2 × McMaster-Carr 60645K121 ball joint rod ends per leg
  • Leg 1B is the mirror of Leg 1A — one design, two legs
  • Full Legs combines both into a single assembly
